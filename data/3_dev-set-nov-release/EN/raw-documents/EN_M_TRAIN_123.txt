Scots anti-immigration group branded 'racist' to stop protests amid 'mounting charges'

A controversial anti-immigration group are to stop holding protests due to "mounting charges and restrictive bail conditions" placed on them. Demonstrations have been held by protesters from the group "Dundee Patriots" at a number of locations across the city in recent months after migrants were placed in various accommodation.

We previously told how residents were left scared to leave their homes following countless rallies near the Reid Square area. The group could be heard chanting "paedo" and "you can stick your Palestine up your a***'" meters away from family homes.

The group have often been seen holding up signs with messages "stop the boats" and "leave our women and children alone". And residents who asked the group to keep the noise down claim they were labelled "paedophile protectors" and verbally abused over megaphones.

Dundee Patriots group member Gav Blues has now confirmed the group will not be attending any more protests. He wrote on Facebook: "Need to let you all know before the lefties find out and post s***e but this is a group decision.

"The Dundee Patriots will not be attending any future protests at this time due to ongoing police harassment, mounting charges, and restrictive bail conditions placed on members of the group. The majority feel it is best to allow matters to settle before considering any future steps.

"That said, I personally will continue to support other communities outside of Dundee (peacefully) where support is needed, welcomed, and genuinely appreciated." The decision comes after a number of members of the Dundee Patriots group were arrested at various protests across the city.

The news comes after a group behind anti-immigration protests at a hotel housing asylum seekers in Falkirk also disbanded after its leader admitted loss of control. Save Our Future & Our Kids’ Futures' (SOF) group started weekly protests outside the Cladhan Hotel in August last year after a migrant who was staying at the hotel was convicting of raping a teenage girl.

Connor Graham, leader of 'Save Our Future & Our Kids’ Futures' (SOF) announced the dissolution of the group via social media on Monday. He claimed the reason was due to the attendance of neo-nazis at their protests.

Tayside Against the Far Right welcomed the news that the group would no longer be protesting, and branded them 'far-right racist thugs' - a label which the group would deny. A spokesperson for Tayside Against the Far Right said: "We always knew it was only a matter of time before this particular group of far-right racist thugs were smashed.

"They were based in Dundee, a predominantly left leaning, working class city who have never accepted Tories and right wing groups, a University city where students from all over world come to study and one of the most welcoming diverse and friendly places in the country. "Regardless of Blues claims, the reality is they were reduced to just two and a few hangers on. They've made fools of themselves time and time again. The mention of 'mounting charges' is them suffering the consequences of their own actions, its that simple, no one else's fault and we've no doubt there are more charges to come.

"We're not celebrating this victory. This particular battle maybe won and for now our new Dundonians and their neighbours will get some peace. However the fight against the far-right, fascists, Nazis and racists continues."

A spokesperson for Stand up to Racism Tayside added: "It is a testament to anti racist activists in Dundee that the 'Dundee Patriots' have crumbled. They were undone by their own hand.

"Since August they have been causing fear and alarm for asylum seekers, young people, local residents and anti racist activists. We are glad to hear that these people will now be safe from intimidation."
