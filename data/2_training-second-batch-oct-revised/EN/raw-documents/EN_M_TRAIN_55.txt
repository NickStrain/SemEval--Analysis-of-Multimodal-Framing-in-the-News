IOF intensifies aggression on Jenin during Eid days

The Israeli occupation forces (IOF) have escalated their military offensive on the city of Jenin and its refugee camps in the northern occupied West Bank during the Eid al-Fitr holiday, amid a 71-day-long incursion that has severely impacted the region. Since the first day of Eid, the IOF carried out violent raids and mass arrests, involving the assault and intimidation of civilians.

According to the Jenin Media Committee, the IOF continued their attacks on the refugee camp, carrying out demolitions, setting homes on fire, and converting others into military outposts. In a statement released Tuesday, the committee reported that additional military reinforcements, bulldozers, and armored vehicles were deployed to Jenin city and its camp on the third day of Eid al-Fitr. The army is reportedly excavating and expanding roads, systematically altering the camp’s geography and demolishing civilian homes.

The humanitarian situation has deteriorated significantly for the 21,000 internally displaced people, as the offensive continues. Thousands of residents have lost their livelihoods, and the siege on Jenin remains in place, further restricting movement and access to essential services.

According to data from the committee, approximately 600 homes have been completely destroyed, and a further 3,250 housing units rendered uninhabitable, in addition to widespread destruction of roads and public infrastructure across the camp. The Israeli military has also expanded its operations to surrounding towns, including Jalboun to the east and Qabatiya to the south, where soldiers have conducted house raids and vandalized property.

The statement also highlighted the daily surge in arrests, including the detention of dozens of young men and the mother of a wanted individual in Qabatiya, though some detainees were later released. So far, the assault on Jenin and its camp has resulted in the killing of 36 Palestinians, including two shot by Palestinian Authority security forces, as well as dozens of injuries and arrests. The ongoing military campaign continues to raise serious concerns over collective punishment, forced displacement, and widespread violations of international humanitarian law, as Jenin remains at the center of one of the most sustained and destructive Israeli operations in the West Bank in recent years.
