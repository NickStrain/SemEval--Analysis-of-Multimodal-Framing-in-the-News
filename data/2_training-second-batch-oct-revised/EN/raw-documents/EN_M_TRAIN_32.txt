Fujairah Crown Prince welcomes World Taekwondo Federation President

FUJAIRAH, 10th May, 2025 (WAM) - H. H. Sheikh Mohammed bin Hamad bin Mohammed Al Sharqi, Crown Prince of Fujairah, received Dr. Choue Chung-won, President of the World Taekwondo Federation, and members of the Federation's Executive Board at Al Rumaila Palace, on the sidelines of the opening of the World Taekwondo Cadet Championships - Fujairah 2025, which kicked off today with the participation of over 800 male and female athletes from around the world. The tournament will continue until May 14.

During the meeting, H. H. affirmed that Fujairah's hosting of international championships in various sports, including taekwondo, stems from a strategic vision aimed at reinforcing Fujairah's status as a global sports hub and providing an inspiring environment for the development of young local and international athletic talent. H. H. noted that these achievements would not have been possible without the guidance and support of H. H. Sheikh Hamad bin Mohammed Al Sharqi, Supreme Council Member and Ruler of Fujairah, who has devoted significant resources to ensure that sports events in the emirate are organized in line with the highest international standards.

He also praised the pioneering role of the World Taekwondo Federation in advancing the sport and organising major global championships. He emphasised that Fujairah's consistent success in hosting sports events reflects its strong commitment to supporting the sports movement and creating an ideal environment for organising international competitions.

For his part, the President of the World Taekwondo Federation expressed his deep appreciation and gratitude to the Crown Prince of Fujairah for his continued support of sports, especially taekwondo, and for his backing of the Federation's efforts to ensure the success of global sporting events. He also commended the Crown Prince's humanitarian support for refugees and athletes worldwide, as well as his initiatives reflecting a deep commitment to the noble values of sport as a means of development and human connection.
