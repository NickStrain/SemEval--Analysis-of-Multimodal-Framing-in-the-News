[EN_M_TRAIN_60#0] Over 300,000 Refugees Return To Syria Since President Assad's Ouster: UN

[EN_M_TRAIN_60#2] Turkey is home to nearly three million refugees who fled Syria after the civil war began in 2011 and is keen to see them return home. [EN_M_TRAIN_60#3] Syria's war killed more than half a million people and displaced millions from their homes. It remains "the world's largest displacement crisis", Schmitt said, stressing that most of those who had fled the war were eager to return to their homes.

[EN_M_TRAIN_60#4] In addition to the returning refugees, she said that another 900,000 people who had been internally displaced inside Syria had gone back to their home areas, "so in total, it's 1.2 million people returned since early December", she said. UNHCR, she said, had conducted a survey indicating that a full one million internally displaced people living in camps and sites across northwestern Syria intend to return home "within the next year".
